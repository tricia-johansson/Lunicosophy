I'm Lucas, the thinker behind Lunicosophy

Lunicosophy is a philosophy that aims to assist marginalized groups by offering frameworks and thought based on minority thinking, and by that can help to better understand the experience of marginalization and being in the minority in society. This is especially helpful to offer inclusion because step one in making that happen is to actually trying to understand the experience!

Lunicosophy got its name from "l'unico" which is Italian for "the only one" and "sophy" which comes from the Greek "sophia" meaning "wisdom". It also comes from its original name "Luna Oculo", which means "Moon's Eye" in Latin, which suggests that this is an occult philosophy as well! You can read more about my reflections on the name on Substack or in my paper "Introduction to Lunicosophy"

And who am I? I'm Lucas, a trans man based in Gothenburg, Sweden. I'm currently doing my Bachelor's in Divinity, but I'm primarily an independent scholar and philosopher, and I mostly study independently.
